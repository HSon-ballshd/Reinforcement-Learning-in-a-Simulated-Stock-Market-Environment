## Primary Objective

Develop a reinforcement learning trading agent capable of interacting with a recreated version of the Cookie Clicker stock market and compare its performance against traditional trading strategies.

## Secondary Objective

Investigate whether hidden market regimes can be inferred from observable price behavior and whether regime information improves reinforcement learning performance.

## Primary Research Question

Can a reinforcement learning agent learn profitable trading behavior in the Cookie Clicker stock market, and how does its performance compare to heuristic-based trading strategies?

## Secondary Research Question

Can hidden market regimes be inferred from observable market data, and does explicit regime inference improve the performance of a reinforcement learning trading agent?

# 3. Environment Design (Updated)

## Hidden Market Regimes

Each stock internally maintains a hidden market mode.

The mode is not directly visible to trading agents.

Possible modes:

0 = Stable

1 = Bullish

2 = Bearish

3 = Strong Bull

4 = Strong Bear

5 = Chaotic

The hidden mode influences future price evolution through drift and volatility adjustments.

This creates a partially observable environment and provides a controlled testbed for latent-state inference.

# 8. Regime Inference Module

## Motivation

The simulator provides access to hidden market modes during data generation but not during trading.

This allows investigation of whether market regimes leave observable signatures in price behavior.

## Dataset Generation

During simulation, the following information will be recorded:

Inputs:

- Current price
- Recent returns
- Rolling averages
- Price volatility
- Price momentum indicators

Labels:

- Hidden market mode

Generated dataset format:

X = observable market features

Y = hidden market mode

---

## Candidate Models

The project will evaluate simple classification models:

- Logistic Regression
- Random Forest
- Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)

Optional:

- LSTM sequence model

The simplest model providing reasonable performance will be preferred.

---

## Evaluation Metrics

Regime prediction performance will be measured using:

- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1 Score
- Confusion Matrix

---

## Research Question

Can hidden market modes be predicted from observable market information significantly better than random guessing?

# 9. Reinforcement Learning Agent (Updated)

Two DQN variants will be evaluated.

## DQN Baseline

Inputs:

- Market state
- Portfolio state

Outputs:

- Trading actions

---

## DQN + Regime Inference

Inputs:

- Market state
- Portfolio state
- Predicted regime probabilities

Outputs:

- Trading actions

The predicted regime probabilities are generated by the regime classifier.

This architecture tests whether explicit latent-state estimation improves trading performance.

# 13. Expected Outcomes (Updated)

Hypothesis H1:

DQN will outperform Random and Buy-and-Hold strategies.

Hypothesis H2:

Hidden market modes can be predicted from observable price behavior at accuracy significantly above random chance.

Hypothesis H3:

Providing inferred regime information to the DQN agent will improve trading performance relative to a standard DQN.

# 14. Academic Contribution

The project contributes in two areas:

1. Reinforcement Learning Trading

Evaluation of a DQN-based trading agent within a simulated market environment.

2. Latent Regime Inference

Investigation of whether hidden market dynamics can be reconstructed from observable information and leveraged to improve decision making.

This transforms the project from a pure trading benchmark into a study of learning and exploiting hidden market structure in a partially observable environment.
